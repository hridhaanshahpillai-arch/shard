THE STORY

Every spring, the Emerald Heart beat once and the valley turned green. This year it cracked, and nine pieces flew up the mountain like shooting stars going the wrong way.

Pip has just turned eighteen and has never climbed higher than the sheep fields. But old Bram gives him a red scarf that remembers the heart's warmth, and Brae is waiting at the edge of the village with a few words for him before he goes...
